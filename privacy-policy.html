Privacy Policy

Valley Water Heater Pros is a lead-generation website serving Tempe, Arizona and the surrounding East Valley. This privacy policy explains what information this site collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

By using this site, you agree to the practices described below. If you do not agree, please do not use the site.

Information This Site Collects

This site collects only the information you choose to share. When you request service, you provide details such as your name, your town or neighborhood, and a description of your water heater problem. That information is needed to match your request with an independent local contractor who serves your area.

Like most websites, this site also collects limited technical information automatically. This includes the type of browser you use, the pages you visit, and approximate location data derived from your IP address. This technical data is used in aggregate to understand how visitors use the site and to improve its content.

This site does not collect payment information. It does not ask for Social Security numbers, financial account details, or other sensitive personal data. Please do not submit that kind of information through any service request.

How Your Information Is Used

Service request details are shared with one or more independent local plumbing contractors so they can contact you about your water heater needs. That is the core function of this site: connecting your request with a contractor who can do the work. Contractors receive only the information relevant to responding to your request.

Aggregate usage data helps improve the site. If many visitors read the tankless services page, for example, that tells us the topic deserves deeper coverage. Technical data is never used to identify you personally.

Your information is not sold to data brokers, and it is not shared with advertisers. It is shared only with contractors for the purpose of responding to your service request, and with service providers that operate this website's infrastructure, such as hosting and analytics, under their own privacy obligations.

Reasonable safeguards are used to protect the information you submit. No method of internet transmission is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed, but this site takes standard precautions to limit access to your details.

Cookies and Your Choices

This site may use cookies, small files stored on your device, for basic functions like remembering site preferences and for analytics that measure which pages visitors find useful. Cookies do not contain personal information you have not provided.

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers let you block or delete cookies, though blocking them may affect how some parts of the site behave. Analytics on this site is configured to collect aggregate trends rather than individual profiles.

If you want to ask about the information you submitted, request a correction, or ask that your service request details be deleted, use the contact page to reach out. Describe your request and the details you provided so it can be located. Applicable privacy laws give you the right to know what personal information is held about you and to request its deletion.

This policy may be updated as the site changes. The version on this page is the current one, and continued use of the site after an update means you accept the revised policy.
